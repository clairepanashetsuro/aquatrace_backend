Title: Turbidity troubleshooting
Source: AquaTrace team summary of WHO guidance (sample)
Version: 1

High turbidity may mean sediment was disturbed after flushing or work on the pipes, or that the sensor is dirty.

- Check sensor health and recent cleaning records.
- Look at the last 24 hours of "readings".
